## 2. Exact Geographic & Topographical Verification of Makerere Kivulu

1. **Exact Location & Boundaries (High Confidence — Mapcarta / OpenStreetMap / World Bank / Wikipedia):**
   - **Makerere Kivulu** (also subdivided into Kivulu I and Kivulu II) is a high-density parish/informal-to-mixed settlement in **Kawempe Division, Kampala**, lying immediately **northwest of central Kampala** in the **valley/slope directly below Makerere Hill**.
   - **Bounding & Adjacent Arterial Corridors:**
     - **West / Northwest:** **Makerere University** main campus & **Makerere Hill Road / Makerere Road** descending toward Wandegeya.
     - **North / Northeast:** **Bombo Road** & **Wandegeya** (historically recognized as the birthplace of the student-driven Ugandan *Rolex*).
     - **South / Southwest:** **Gaddafi Road (Gadafi Rd)**, the **Law Development Centre (LDC)** campus on Makerere Hill perimeter, **Kagugube**, and **Old Kampala**; **Appolo Kigenza Road** / tertiary access lanes descend toward Bat Valley and Nakasero.
   - **Hydrology & Slope (Important Correction to Preliminary Model):**
     - World Bank watershed documentation notes that **natural springs / drainage channels originate right between Bat Valley, Wandegeya, and Makerere Kivulu**, feeding southward into the Nakivubo drainage network.
     - Thus, Kivulu is **NOT a flat desert/coastal plain**: it sits on a **distinct hillside-to-valley gradient**, with Makerere Hill rising steeply on one side and surface drainage trenches cutting along the lower lanes.
